
# About Me
Hi, everyone! My name is Zhong Dengpeng. I was born in 2007 and come from Yueyang, Hunan. I currently live in Yulin City, Guangxi. I have lived in Yulin for many years, completed my compulsory education here, and am now enrolled in a full-time key high school. However, I am currently on leave due to health reasons.

## Hobbies
I enjoy radio, trains, and aviation. Trains were my earliest passion, followed by aviation, which became an interest during elementary school. As for radio, I stumbled upon it in the fifth grade when I happened to come across a video by Mr. BG6FQO (a.k.a. Science Xiaowang) on Bilibili. It was through his content that I learned about this community and developed a deep interest in this traditional communication method.

- Amateur Radio

After developing an interest in radio in fifth grade, I started learning more about it online and passed the Class A operator's license in 2023. In 2024, with the introduction of new regulations for amateur radio in China that allow minors to independently set up and operate amateur radio stations, I began engaging in transmission activities, such as local repeater communications and satellite communications. Of these, satellite communications have become my favorite method, as I can connect with many distant stations, including international ones from Japan, the Philippines, Thailand, Indonesia, and others. Due to limited equipment and funds, I can currently only participate in a few types of communications. In the future, I plan to explore more communication methods and legal frequency bands. My short-term goal is the 6M band FT8.

- Railways

I have a strong passion for railway photography and often travel with friends to other places to capture unique railway landscapes and local train models. Each trip brings me great joy, and taking satisfying photos gives me immense satisfaction. I post some of my favorite photography works on my blog. Please be kind when viewing them!

- Aviation

My love and understanding of aviation are not as deep as my other two hobbies. I have only a basic knowledge of aviation and occasionally engage in aviation operations. As for why I don’t do aviation photography, the simple reason is that I can’t afford the necessary camera equipment (bitter smile).
